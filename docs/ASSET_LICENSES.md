# Ayna bootstrap assets

The procedural magenta octahedron returned by `dummy_glb()` and `backend/fixtures/dummy.glb`
is original Ayna bootstrap geometry dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0.
It is a dummy fixture, not a reconstructed or scanned product. The procedural PNG browser
test photographs are also CC0-1.0. No customer images or third-party product photographs
are bundled. The Ayna mark in `frontend/public/favicon.svg` is original code-native art.

preserved in the container at `/usr/share/doc/minio/LICENSE`. Source:
https://github.com/minio/minio/tree/7aac2a2c5b7c882e68c1ce017d8256be2feea27f
